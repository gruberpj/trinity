This book was derived from retreat talks given at three retreats in February and March of 2025.

Beginning with St. Patrick's shamrock and Rublev's icon, these chapters treat the Trinity not as a puzzle to be solved but as a mystery to be entered — so knowable that it overwhelms our ability to know. From image, the retreats move through gift, liturgy, relationship, intimacy, and out into evangelization — an invitation into the very life of Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, the love that made the sun and the stars.

The book closes with a talk Fr. Peter gave at a retreat for engineering students, where the mystery speaks to the modern technical mind. Fr. Peter Gruber, C.O., is a priest of the Oratory of St. Philip Neri serving university students in Pittsburgh.
