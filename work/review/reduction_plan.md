- **Splice note** says what remains before/after so the paragraph still flows. One-word notes like "None" mean deleting the span leaves adjacent sentences coherent.
- **Executor moves every cut** (verbatim) into `chapters/outlines/parking_lot.md` under a new `## Reduction 2026-09-16` section, one entry per cut (chapter, span, reason). No chapter file is edited by this plan.
