## B. Developments (how themes build across the talks)

1. **Mystery grows hands-on:** ch. 1 gives images to grip (shamrock, icon, sign) → ch. 2 defines mystery and implicates the self → ch. 4 adds a second kind of knowing (connatural) → ch. 5 moves from knowing to indwelling (*intimus*) → ch. 6 hands the mystery outward (evangelization).
2. **Gift becomes circulation:** ch. 3 names creation as gift and liturgy as response, and introduces the feedback loop of grace → ch. 6 runs the loop outward through other Christs → epilogue applies it to the individual's self-offering (GS 24, widow's mite, morning offering).
3. **The sign of the cross deepens:** ch. 1 reads its words and motions → ch. 4 finds it as the cruciform baptismal seal reenacted → ch. 5 receives it as "a hug from the Holy Trinity" (an audience member's phrase the speaker adopts — the retreat's signature image arc).
4. **The "we" progresses:** image (ch. 1) → implicated (ch. 2) → respondent (ch. 3) → relational (ch. 4) → indwelt (ch. 5) → sent (ch. 6).
5. **Benedict/Ratzinger spans the retreat:** graveyard warning (ch. 2) → creation-love attribution (ch. 3, unresolved) → friendship homily (ch. 4) → fifth transcendental + thought of God (ch. 5) → "nothing more beautiful" (ch. 6) → "made for greatness" (epilogue, unverified).
