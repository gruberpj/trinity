## 1. Characteristic sentence length

Two registers alternate deliberately, and both are load-bearing:

- **Short declarative sentences (2–10 words), used as punchlines, definitions, and paragraph landings.**
  - "The Trinity is a mystery. We can't understand it. It's there." (4/5/2 words; C02 00:01:32)
  - "Everything is positive. Everything is good." (3/3; C03 00:12:47)
  - "The words matter." (3; C01 00:18:10)
  - "Liturgy is this response." (4; C03 00:08:24)
  - "Negative feedback loops are safe." (5; E01 00:34:56)
  - "And that's a helpful thing. It's wrong. It's a false etymology." (C05 00:12:39)
  - "Why something rather than nothing? Liturgy." (C03 00:08:24)
- **Long pastoral runs (25–45 words), used for theological explanation and story-telling, typically followed by a short landing sentence.**
  - "There are pieces of information that are not there, or if they are present, they require us to deduce from the material given to arrive at the conclusion." (28 words; C02 00:01:32)
  - "Entering not only into our humanity, but taking all of our sufferings onto himself, all of our misery, all of our sin — the descent of Christ into the depths of human misery, so that all human suffering is taken on him." (~41 words; C01 00:18:46)
  - "It is so far away that any change in our distance here at 60 or 70 miles per hour — you will not be able to notice any change in position in the sky relative to you." (~35 words; C02 00:09:04)
- **Sentence-initial discourse markers are habitual:** "And", "But", "So", "Well", "Now", "Okay", "Right?" — e.g. "And that's God." (standalone; C05 00:01:06), "So St. Patrick did not shy from holding up a shamrock…" (C01 00:01:42), "Well, that's actually a bad translation, or a different translation." (C06 00:10:04).
- **Em-dash asides and audible self-correction are kept in the clean layer** — the voice revises mid-sentence instead of pre-editing: "we have the red of — it's kind of in between" (C01 00:10:44); "I'm going to — I've said anything, so I'm sorry" (E01 00:28:33).
- **Drafting rule:** never flatten to one sentence length. The rhythm is *long build-up → short landing*, and the short sentence carries the thesis.

## 3. First/second person usage and direct address

- **"We" is the default and dominant pronoun** for doctrine and application alike: "we are implicated", "we are enveloped", "we are images of God", "we are meant to". The audience is assumed *inside* the mystery, not lectured from outside.
- **"You" appears for audience actions and invitations:** "take it out and look at it" (C01 00:03:46), "I recommend taking this to the chapel" (C01 00:16:40), "do feel free to share" (C01 00:16:40), "you guys" (C01, E01). In E01 the register shifts toward a more familiar "you guys" / "Have you guys studied any systems engineering yet?" (E01 00:34:56).
- **"I" is reserved for testimony and self-deprecation**, concentrated in E01 (autobiography: the Pitt decision, Uncle Mark, his mother's "Oh, but you were such a good engineer.") and in the set-piece stories ("I'll tell a story to illustrate this point", C02 00:08:27).
- **Direct-address patterns:**
  - Rhetorical question + immediate answer: "Who are we crossing? Ourselves." (C01 00:22:52); "why this chair? For sitting. Why food? For eating. Why something rather than nothing? Liturgy." (C03 00:08:24).
  - Imperatives as application landings: "Give thanks." (C03 00:22:56); "Go forth." (C06); "do not hide this away" (C06 00:12:53); "Welcome him. Speak to him. Be silent with him." (E01 00:44:04).
  - Soft invitations: "I recommend…", "do feel free to share", "what I recommend to do — this is to live this life of thanksgiving" (C03 00:22:56).
  - Meta-commentary on the talk itself, keeping the room present: "We're at 21 minutes and I'm sorry. But you're permitted to groan." (C01 00:20:55).

## 6. Prayerful register

- **Every talk opens:** Sign of the Cross → a set prayer → Sign of the Cross.
  - C01: final part of St. Patrick's Breastplate ("I bind unto myself the name…")
  - C02: Elizabeth of the Trinity — "O my God, Trinity, whom I adore, help me to forget myself entirely…"
  - C03: Psalm 42 ("As a deer longs for flowing streams…")
  - C04: Elizabeth of the Trinity — "O eternal Word, Word of my God… O my three, my all, my beatitude…"
  - C05: Augustine — "Late have I loved you, O Beauty ever ancient, ever new…"
  - C06: Newman's "Dear Jesus, help me to spread your fragrance everywhere I go…" (the "Radiating Christ" prayer)
  - E01: a custom prayer to the Blessed Virgin + Hail Mary
- **Every talk closes:** Sign of the Cross → Glory Be → Sign of the Cross. E01 adds "Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest." (Mt 11:28) just before the closing, then "Thank you, everyone."
- **Mid-talk eruptions — exhortation turns into prayer at the climax:**
  - C03 returns to the opening psalm: "Deep calls to deep… From the abyss of our hearts, we call out to the abyss of the Holy Trinity." then "And we say, let this happen. Let deep call out unto deep. Let me make this prayer of hope." (C03 00:21:03)
  - C06: "Let us not hold that back. Let's open the floodgates of God's mercy and love into this fallen world." (C06 00:14:44); closing "Let Christ love us. Let him love others through us." (C06 00:15:12)
  - C05: "Let him love you. Let him embrace you. Let him hug you. And respond by welcoming him in your hearts…" (C05 00:22:29)
  - E01: "Welcome him. Speak to him. Be silent with him. Gaze on his face with wonder." (E01 00:44:04)
- Prayer is also the *application*: "the proper Christian response to mystery is to love, adore, and worship" (C02 00:17:47); "I recommend taking this to the chapel" (C01 00:16:40); "to be thankful before God at all times, in all places" (C03 00:22:56).

## 7. Typical movement (doctrine → lived application)

Recurring arc, all seven talks:

1. **Concrete image, object, or story** (shamrock, icon, sun, table, moon, Easter fire, Giant Eagle, his own biography).
2. **A term re-defined by contrast**, almost always a "not X, but Y" move: mystery is not a puzzle to solve; liturgy is not rubrics; God does not *have* being, he *is* being; *missa est* does not mean "the Mass has ended" but "it has been sent"; intimacy is not "into me see" but *intimus*.
3. **Authority chain** (Scripture → Church → named thinkers): Ratzinger/Benedict, Newman, Augustine, Thérèse, Elizabeth of the Trinity, Giussani, Marcel, Buber, Fagerberg, Weber, Chesterton, C.S. Lewis, John Paul II, John of the Cross, de Montfort, Maritain, St. Patrick. Attributions are usually oral ("as Pope Benedict says", "I read somewhere", "according to Bill Daniels, I think, and according to Joseph Ratzinger" — C03 00:01:11).
4. **Analogy from physics, engineering, family, or domestic life** (see §5).
5. **The application lands as "we are meant to…" / "made for…", an imperative, or an invitation** — then frequently tips into prayer or a bridge to the next talk:
   - C01 ends with the cliffhanger: "how this changes not just how we approach our faith but everything in our life, we will discuss in the future conferences." (C01 00:23:47)
   - C03 ends with the single recommendation: "And what I recommend to do — this is to live this life of thanksgiving… Saint Paul says, 'Give thanks.'" (C03 00:22:56)
   - C06: "If this is a secret, tell everyone about this." → prayer. (C06 00:12:15)
   - E01: "The interior life is a response to the invitation that Christ constantly gives us." → Mt 11:28. (E01 00:44:04)
- The movement is **doctrine → "we are implicated" → response**. He never leaves a doctrine unapplied, and never applies without having first given the theology room to run long.

## 9. Restraints for drafting (anti-caricature)

1. **Keep the asides and meta-commentary.** Do not smooth away "We're at 21 minutes and I'm sorry", "Don't ask me how I know", "You see how that worked out?". These are the voice, not noise.
2. **Do not uniformize sentence length.** Preserve the long-run → short-landing rhythm, the sentence-initial And/But/So/Well/Now/Okay, and the em-dash self-corrections. A paragraph of all-medium sentences is generic AI prose.
3. **Do not add theological jargon he does not use.** `perichoresis` and `consubstantial` have **zero occurrences** in any transcript. He says "subsistent relations" only 3 times and glosses it. If the source DOCX supplies a term the audio never uses, mark it [VERIFY] in the provenance notes rather than putting it in his mouth.
4. **Keep the hedges.** "kind of", "sort of", "as it were", "in some way or other", "I believe that's the word", "I read somewhere", "Some would say". Removing them makes him falsely certain.
5. **Do not invent quotes, stories, jokes, or citations.** Every quoted line traces to a clean-transcript timestamp. Every story (moon, eclipse, Giant Eagle, MySpace Tom) already exists — new stories are forbidden.
6. **Story variants must not be merged.** The moon story exists in two tellings (C02, E01); the waiting-friend analogy in two (C05 with Giant Eagle, E01 with the couch); the relationship litany in two (C04 "Motherhood expresses it… when it does not despair, adores it" vs. E01 "Motherhood embraces it… does not give in to despair — adores it"). Draft the variant that belongs to the chapter being drafted; do not "correct" one against the other.
7. **Preserve attributions, especially adopted audience material.** "The sign of the cross is a hug from the Holy Trinity" is credited to an audience member; keep the credit.
8. **Do not make him aphoristic.** The one-liners work because they are earned after long meditative build-up. A text of stacked one-liners is a caricature. Likewise, do not make him breezy: the register is warm but slow and doctrinal.
9. **Keep pastoral directness:** "you guys" (sparingly), imperatives ("Give thanks."), invitations ("I recommend", "do feel free to share"), and the "we" that includes the audience in the mystery.
10. **E01 register is not the C01–C06 register.** E01 is autobiographical, engineering-laden, and familiar ("you guys", "spiritual life hack"). Do not blend it wholesale into the six conference chapters; use it for its unique material only.
11. **Keep the load-bearing formulas:** "we are meant to", "made for", "the love that made the sun and the stars", "teeming with", "overwhelms", "implicated", "enveloped". Do not synonym-swap them.
12. **Keep his Trinitarian trailing-amplification habit:** "not to separate… but to actually connect us"; "He does not have, he is."
13. **Preserve `[possibly: ...]` / `[unclear ...]` flags** wherever quoted material carries them (see C04 exemplar above).
14. **Do not modernize or "improve" doctrinal formulations** (handoff rule). Convert spoken punctuation, not theology.
15. **Application must follow doctrine.** Do not excerpt the application without the theology that earned it, or vice versa.
