## Heard wording (clean transcript)
Late have I loved you, Beauty ever ancient, ever new — late have I loved you!

## Evidence — verbatim from fetched sources
Latin: sero te amavi, pulchritudo tam antiqua et tam nova, sero te amavi! et ecce intus eras et ego foris... ICEL Liturgy of the Hours (Evening Prayer antiphon, Memorial of St. Augustine, 28 Aug): Late have I loved you, O Beauty ever ancient, ever new, late have I loved you. You called, you shouted and you shattered my darkness. (Office of Readings: ...late have I loved you! You were within me, but I was outside...) Pusey: Too late did I love You, O Fairness, so ancient, and yet so new! Too late did I love You!

## Discrepancy / comparison notes
Heard wording matches the ICEL Liturgy of the Hours rendering ('Beauty ever ancient, ever new') minus the 'O'; it differs from Pusey ('O Fairness, so ancient, and yet so new'). Latin verified. If printed as a quotation, either cite the LOTH wording (ICEL © — permission) or a public-domain translation (Pusey).
