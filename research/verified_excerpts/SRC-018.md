## Full-range comparison (performed against RSV text above; footnote markers omitted)
The entire 45-verse range was compared verse by verse. It is RSV verbatim throughout **except**:
- **14:16** DOCX: "And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Counselor…" — RSV and RSVCE(1966): "And I will **pray** the Father, and he will give you another Counselor…"
- **14:31** DOCX: "Rise, let us go **from here**." — RSV: "Rise, let us go **hence**."

## Spot-check verses (RSV, verbatim)
- 14:2-3 "In my Father's house are many rooms; if it were not so, would I have told you that I go to prepare a place for you? 3 And when I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again and will take you to myself, that where I am you may be also."
- 14:16-17 "And I will pray the Father, and he will give you another Counselor, to be with you for ever, 17 even the Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot receive, because it neither sees him nor knows him; you know him, for he dwells with you, and will be in you."
- 15:12-13 "This is my commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you. 13 Greater love has no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends."

## Comparison notes
- Distinctive RSV readings throughout confirm the RSV family: "rooms" (not "dwelling places"), "Counselor" (not "Advocate"/"Helper"), "vinedresser", "he prunes", "cast forth as a branch and withers".
- The two deviations ("ask", "from here") match neither RSV nor RSVCE (1966); they may reflect the RSV Second Catholic Edition (Ignatius, 2006) or authorial modernization — unverifiable from free sources. Human confirmation of the exact edition is required.
