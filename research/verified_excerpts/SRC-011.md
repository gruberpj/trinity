## Authoritative excerpt (CCC ¶260, vatican.va verbatim — the verified rendering)
"O my God, Trinity whom I adore, help me forget myself entirely so to establish myself in you, unmovable and peaceful as if my soul were already in eternity. May nothing be able to trouble my peace or make me leave you, O my unchanging God, but may each minute bring me more deeply into your mystery! Grant my soul peace. Make it your heaven, your beloved dwelling and the place of your rest. May I never abandon you there, but may I be there, whole and entire, completely vigilant in my faith, entirely adoring, and wholly given over to your creative action."

Footnote 102: "Prayer of Blessed Elizabeth of the Trinity." (Vatican page predates her 2016 canonization; newer print editions read "Saint".)

## Comparison notes
- The DOCX standalone version ("as still and as peaceful", "O my Unchanging One", "wholly surrendered to Your creative Action", capitalized You/Your) matches NONE of the located published translations: not the CCC/Kane text, not the Dijon Carmel / O.Carm rendering ("let me entirely forget myself that I may abide in you…"), not Stanbrook 1914 ("as changeless and as calm… O my immutable Lord"). It appears to be a modernized devotional derivative (closest affinities: Stanbrook's "as Adorer, as Restorer, as Savior" and "abyss of Your greatness").
