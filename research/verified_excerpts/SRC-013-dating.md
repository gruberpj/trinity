# SRC-013-dating — Rublev "Trinity": dating and current location

- **Accessed:** 2026-09-16
- **Status:** VERIFIED — facts quoted verbatim from live fetches on 2026-09-16 (own fetches + pipeline subagent fetches of the same date, each noted). The tretyakovgallery.ru official collection page and Britannica could not be fetched (flagged below).

## Recommended phrasing for the book (ONE sentence)

The Trinity of Andrei Rublev (c. 1411 or 1425–1427; State Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow — since 2023–2024 in Russian Orthodox Church custody and displayed at the Trinity Cathedral of the Trinity Lavra of St. Sergius, Sergiyev Posad).

## Dating evidence (two scholarly camps — conflict unresolved)

Scholarship is split between two datings; the museum itself uses a dual dating.

1. **c. 1411–1412 (icon made for the wooden church of Abbot Nikon, after the 1411 fire; camp: V. N. Lazarev, G. V. Popov, E. S. Smirnova).** Russian Wikipedia infobox: "Андрей Рублёв «Троица» . 1412 год или 20-е года XV века" — https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Троица_(икона_Рублёва) (accessed 2026-09-16, verified directly).
2. **1420s, i.e. 1422–1427 / 1425–1428 (icon made for the stone Trinity Cathedral, founded 1422; camp: V. I. Antonova, E. K. Guseva, N. A. Demina, G. I. Vzdornov, M. A. Ilyin, T. N. Manushina).** Same article (verified directly): "другие исследователи относят икону к 20-м годам XV века, связывая происхождение этого образа с каменным монастрыским собором, который был заложен в 1422 году (В. И. Антонова, Э. К. Гусева, Н. А. Дёмина, Г. И. Вздорнов, М. А. Ильин, Т. Н. Манушина и др.) [32]. Датировка Третьяковской галереи — между 1422 и 1427 годами [33]. Искусствоведы, рассматривая икону с точки зрения стиля, не считают этот вопрос окончательно решённым. И. Э. Грабарь осторожно датировал «Троицу» 1408—1425 годами, Ю. А. Лебедева — 1422—1423, В. И. Антонова — 1420—1427 годами." (first sentence of this passage — naming the Lazarev camp — fetched by pipeline subagent 2026-09-16; the remainder verified directly 2026-09-16.)
3. **Tretyakov Gallery Magazine catalog page** — https://www.tg-m.ru/catalog/en/picture/22115 (accessed 2026-09-16, verified directly): "The Holy Trinity Rublev Andrei 1425-1427 Levkas and tempera on wood. 142 × 114 cm Музей: Tretyakov Gallery".
4. **Google Arts & Culture asset page** — https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/the-holy-trinity-icon-andrei-rublev/dwF-zpb9JXF6Kg (fetched by pipeline subagent 2026-09-16): metadata "Date: 1411/1427".
5. **English Wikipedia** "Trinity (Andrei Rublev)" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_(Andrei_Rublev) (fetched by pipeline subagent 2026-09-16): infobox "Year 1411 or 1425–27"; body: "The official version states 1411 or 1425–27."

Conclusion: the safest book phrasing is the dual range "c. 1411 or 1425–1427" (matching the museum's public-facing dual dating); "1410s–1420s" is an acceptable looser alternative.

## Location evidence 2023–2026

1. **May 2023 — transfer decision.** TASS, 17 May 2023, "Andrey Rublev's 'Holy Trinity' icon may disintegrate upon changing hands — museum director", https://tass.com/society/1618731 (fetched by pipeline subagent 2026-09-16): "The Moscow Patriarchate reported on its website on May 15 that Andrey Rublev's 'Holy Trinity' icon would be returned to the custody of the Russian Orthodox Church (ROC) by order of President Vladimir Putin. On May 16, the Russian Ministry of Culture's press service confirmed that the celebrated 15th century icon would be put on display at Moscow's Cathedral of Christ the Savior on Orthodox Pentecost Sunday, which falls on June 4 this year. After that, it will be restored and returned to its traditional place as the centerpiece of the iconostasis (wall of icons in an Eastern Orthodox church) in the Trinity Cathedral at the Trinity Lavra of St. Sergius monastery in the town of Sergiev Posad, 70 kilometers northeast of Moscow."
2. **June 2023 — display at the Cathedral of Christ the Saviour, Moscow.** The Moscow Times, 5 June 2023, "In Photos: Renowned Orthodox Icon Displayed at Moscow Cathedral", https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2023/06/05/in-photos-renowned-orthodox-icon-displayed-at-moscow-cathedral-a81401 (fetched by pipeline subagent 2026-09-16): "Russia's most famous icon and one of the most important works in Russian art history, Andrei Rublev's 'Trinity,' has been put on display at Moscow's Christ the Savior Cathedral despite significant controversy." … "The icon was unveiled at Christ the Savior Cathedral on the holiday of Trinity Sunday."
3. **22–23 June 2024 — installation in the Trinity Cathedral iconostasis.** RIA Novosti, 23 June 2024, "Икону 'Троица' установили в Троицком соборе на ее историческом месте", https://ria.ru/20240623/troitsa-1954812346.html (accessed 2026-09-16, verified directly): "Икону 'Троица' Андрея Рублева установили в специальный киот, вмонтированный в иконостас Троицкого собора Троице-Сергиевой лавры, на месте, где она исторически располагалась, сообщил корреспондент РИА Новости. … Накануне икону доставили в Троицкий собор из столичного храма Христа Спасителя. … После почти векового перерыва икона 'Троица', которая была написана Андреем Рублевым для Троицкого собора Троице-Сергиевой лавры, вернулась в этот храм, где будет пребывать постоянно."
4. **12–14 September 2026 — display-case failure; icon removed for restoration; ownership remains with the Tretyakov Gallery.** Meduza, 14 September 2026, https://meduza.io/en/feature/2026/09/14/museum-experts-warned-that-moving-andrei-rublev-s-fragile-trinity-icon-to-a-russian-monastery-could-destroy-it-three-years-later-its-condition-is-reportedly-critical (accessed 2026-09-16, verified directly): "On September 12, Ekho reported that Andrei Rublev's Trinity, housed for the past three years at the Trinity Lavra of St. Sergius rather than in a museum, was in critical condition after its climate-controlled display case broke down. The next day, the Moscow Patriarchate said Tretyakov Gallery staff would examine the icon." … "On September 13, the Moscow Patriarchate said the icon had been removed from the iconostasis and Tretyakov Gallery specialists were conducting a 'routine inspection.'" … "On September 14, the Tretyakov Gallery's press office told TASS that the Trinity was undergoing restoration and confirmed that museum staff would examine the icon's condition. Formally, the Trinity remains part of the Tretyakov Gallery's collection: In 2023, the Russian Orthodox Church received the right to use the icon free of charge for 49 years, with an option to extend the arrangement, rather than acquiring ownership of it."

As of 2026-09-16: the icon is at the Trinity Lavra of St. Sergius (Sergiyev Posad), temporarily removed from the iconostasis for restoration/examination after the display-case failure; the State Tretyakov Gallery retains ownership; the ROC holds a 49-year right of use.
