## Heard wording (clean transcript)
You have made us for yourselves, O Lord, and our hearts are restless till they rest in thee.

## Evidence — verbatim from fetched sources
Latin: fecisti nos ad te et inquietum est cor nostrum donec requiescat in te. Pusey: You have made us for Yourself, and our hearts are restless until they rest in You. ICEL LOTH Morning Prayer antiphon (St. Augustine, 28 Aug): You inspire us, O Lord, to delight in praising you, because you have made us for yourself; our hearts are restless until they rest in you.

## Discrepancy / comparison notes
Heard wording mixes forms: 'yourselves' (no printed edition uses the plural) with archaic 'till...thee' (closest to the traditional/LOTH register). No single edition matches exactly; Latin and both principal English renderings verified.
