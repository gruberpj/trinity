## Heard wording (clean transcript)
One thing that gets in the way of this habit of living in mystery... is what the author of The Soul of the Apostolate calls dissipation. We ought to have the custody of our hearts...

## Evidence — verbatim from fetched sources
(1946 Gethsemani trans., Eighth Truth, pp. 17-18) My interior life will be no better than my custody of my heart. 'Before all things keep a guard over thy heart, for from it springs forth life'... This custody of the heart is simply a HABITUAL or at least frequent anxiety to preserve all my acts, as they arise, from everything that might spoil their motive or their execution. (pp. 18-19) Custody of the heart demands a certain amount of recollection: there is no place for it in a soul given to dissipation. (ch. heading, p. 267) Custody of the Heart Is the Keystone of the Interior Life...

## Discrepancy / comparison notes
Book says 'custody of the/my heart,' not the heard 'custody of our hearts.' Both key terms (custody of the heart; dissipation) verified in the 1946 translation.
