# SRC-029-date — Thérèse "Everything is a grace": recommended date

- **Source:** *Novissima Verba: the last conversations and confidences of Saint Thérèse of the Child Jesus, May–September, 1897* (records of Mother Agnès of Jesus), English edition, Burns Oates & Washbourne, 1952 — archive.org scan `novissima-verba-saint-therese-of-lisieux`

- **Locator:** entry headed "5 June 1897"

## Recommended date for the book

**5 June 1897** — per *Novissima Verba* (1952 English edition), the only source directly verified in this environment. If a date is printed, prefer "5 June 1897" with a source note; the commonly cited "11 June 1897" could not be confirmed from any fetched source and must be checked against the French critical edition before use.

## Evidence: 1952 edition placement (verified directly from the scan's djvu.txt, accessed 2026-09-16)

The passage sits under the date heading "5 June 1897":

> "5 June 1897 … 'If you should find me dead some morning, do not be in the least disturbed; it will only mean that papa le bon Dieu has come to fetch me. Without doubt it is a great grace to receive the Sacraments, but when it does not please God to grant this favor, that is equally a grace... . Everything is a grace.'"

Word-count verification of the full 1952 scan text (djvu.txt, accessed 2026-09-16):
- "Everything is a grace" — 1 occurrence (the sentence above)
- "5 June 1897" — 3 occurrences
- "11 June 1897" — **0 occurrences** (the string appears nowhere in the 1952 edition)
- "4 June 1897" — 2 occurrences; "6 June 1897" — 1 occurrence

## The "11 June 1897" attribution

The date 11 June 1897 does not occur anywhere in the 1952 edition. Its source therefore lies in a different edition — presumably the French critical edition of the *Cahier jaune* ("yellow notebook") of Mother Agnès of Jesus, *Derniers Entretiens* (Éditions du Cerf/Desclée de Brouwer, 1971; *Oeuvres complètes*, 1992), and/or the critical English translation *St. Thérèse of Lisieux: Her Last Conversations* (trans. John Clarke, ICS Publications, 1977 — product page confirmed to exist via icspublications.org search, accessed 2026-09-16, https://www.icspublications.org/products/st-therese-of-lisieux-her-last-conversations). **Neither date's authority could be checked in this environment**: archives-carmel-lisieux.fr (Cloudflare 403), Google Books API (HTTP 429 quota exceeded), DuckDuckGo/Mojeek/scholar.archive.org (bot challenges), Bing (no organic results), HathiTrust (HTTP 403), archive.org search-inside for the 1992 *Derniers entretiens* item `derniersentretie0000ther` (HTTP 403 "Item not available"), Wayback CDX API (HTTP 503 offline) — all attempted 2026-09-16.

## Reason for the discrepancy

Not determinable from fetched sources; do not print an explanation. Inference for the human check only (clearly labeled, NOT a finding): the 1952 edition may consolidate notebook sayings under a single date heading, and the critical edition may date "Tout est grâce" to its manuscript position in the yellow notebook (an 11 June entry) — this hypothesis is unverified and must be confirmed or dropped against the critical edition.

## French original wording

"Tout est grâce." is the standard citation of the French original, but the French wording could not be verified verbatim from any fetched source in this environment (the 1952 scan contains no French text).
