# RSV-2CE verification plan (book scripture citations)

- **Prepared:** 2026-09-16
- **Policy:** Quoted scripture should match RSV-2CE (Ignatius Press, 2006) unless the spoken text deviates substantially.
- **Scope:** Ledger rows SRC-014…SRC-019 and SRC-046…SRC-061 (SRC-055 excluded — it is a de Montfort reference, not a scripture row).
- **Constraint:** RSV-2CE text is not freely available online. This plan records **citations only** plus how to obtain and check the text legally. No RSV-2CE verse wording is reproduced here except the two SUSPECTED John 14 readings in §3, taken from the ledger's own finding.

## 3. Suspected RSV-2CE differences (ledger's own finding — these two ONLY)

From SRC-018 (John 14:1–15:12), the book text deviates from both RSV and RSV-CE (1966) in exactly two places. These may reflect RSV-2CE (Ignatius, 2006) — **SUSPECTED / UNCONFIRMED**, to be checked against a licensed RSV-2CE copy:

1. **John 14:16** — book text reads "I will **ask** the Father" — RSV / RSV-CE (1966) read "I will **pray** the Father". SUSPECTED RSV-2CE reading: "ask" (UNCONFIRMED).
2. **John 14:31** — book text reads "Rise, let us go **from here**." — RSV reads "Rise, let us go **hence**." SUSPECTED RSV-2CE reading: "from here" (UNCONFIRMED).

**No other RSV-2CE differences may be assumed.** Every other verse in §2 must be compared fresh against a licensed RSV-2CE text. In particular, the DOCX modernizations recorded for SRC-014/016/017/019/053/056 ("a deer", "you are", "when behold", "and behold", "heavily burdened", etc.) are ledger observations of the *book vs RSV/RSV-CE*, not confirmed RSV-2CE readings — they may coincide with RSV-2CE or may be authorial modernization; only a licensed comparison decides.

## 4. Recommended workflow (staged)

**Stage 0 — obtain one licensed RSV-2CE copy (one-time).**
Cheapest legal options found (2026-09-16): Verbum/Logos module $9.99 (https://verbum.com/product/29627/… or https://www.logos.com/product/29627/…); Catholic Study Bible App free tier (Google Play `org.lighthousecatholicmedia.lcmbible` / App Store id563273711) shows the full Bible text; print: Ignatius Bible 2nd Ed. hardback ISBN 9780898708332 or leather ISBN 9780898709360 (ignatius.com). Any one of these is sufficient for all checks below.

**Stage 1 — decide the edition FIRST (gate check).**
The author (or pipeline runner with licensed access) confirms the two John 14 readings (§3) against RSV-2CE. This single check decides whether the book's scripture is RSV-2CE at all:
- If both match ("ask", "from here") → proceed with RSV-2CE as the book's citation edition.
- If not → the DOCX wording is authorial modernization; the book policy decision (print RSV-2CE vs. keep authorial wording with attribution) goes back to the author + citation/rights reviewer (unresolved.md item F).

**Stage 2 — full-range comparison of the long excerpt.**
SRC-018 (John 14:1–15:12, 45 verses) checked verse-by-verse against RSV-2CE; record per-verse confirmation of citations only. **No verse text is typed into shared files** beyond what the ledger and SRC-018.md already hold. The 45-verse excerpt remains **PERMISSION_REVIEW**: RSV-2CE is © the Division of Christian Education of the NCC (§1.4) — attribution is required, and a 45-verse continuous excerpt needs permission review (already flagged in the ledger).

**Stage 3 — the DOCX modernized rows.**
SRC-014, SRC-016, SRC-017, SRC-019, SRC-053, SRC-056 (and the "this age" blend at SRC-046): compare the DOCX wording against RSV-2CE to learn which apparent modernizations are actually RSV-2CE readings and which are authorial. Record outcomes as ledger-style notes; never paste RSV-2CE verse text.

**Stage 4 — paraphrase rows (policy check only).**
SRC-047, SRC-048, SRC-049, SRC-051, SRC-052, SRC-054, SRC-058, SRC-059, SRC-060, SRC-061 are kept-as-heard paraphrases; RSV-2CE comparison is optional — only verify that the *citation* (book/chapter/verse) still points at the right passage.

**Stage 5 — recording & owners.**
- Add a per-row confirmation field to the ledger rows (e.g., `rsv2ce_confirmed: yes/no/pending` + date + confirmer), never the verse text.
- **Author confirms** each passage against RSV-2CE (print/app); **pipeline cross-checks** when licensed access is available (Verbum/Logos module or CSB app).
- Any correction to printed wording flows through the existing draft/provenance pipeline; ledger, unresolved.md, and other files are not modified by this plan itself.

**Permissions note (repeated for emphasis):** RSV-2CE text © 2000 and 2006 Division of Christian Education, NCC (USA) — attribution per §1.4 required wherever scripture is quoted; the 45-verse John 14:1–15:12 excerpt needs explicit permission review before printing (ledger status PERMISSION_REVIEW).

**Open discrepancy for human review:** book policy says "RSV-2CE (Ignatius Press, 2006)"; ignatius.com product pages list publication date June 7, 2005 for the print editions; Verbum/Logos list "Ignatius, 2006"; the book's own copyright line is "© 2000 and 2006". Do not silently pick one — let the rights/citation reviewer record the edition statement.
