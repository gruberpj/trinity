# SRC-068 — Cardinal Francis Arinze, "sell your coat and buy a catechism" anecdote

- **Heard wording** (first-retreat transcript, liturgy Q&A; mine T5-S9): "There was a Jamaican nun. One time she came to give a talk at the Newman Center. She quoted a cardinal — Cardinal Arinze, who was an African cardinal — who said that if I see a man with a coat but not the Catechism, I tell that man, 'Sell your coat and buy a catechism.'" (The speaker immediately hedges: "So I don't know if that's how Cardinal Arinze talks or if that's how the Jamaican nun talks." The mine notes Arinze is Nigerian and adds: "Anecdote unverified — his hedge must be kept verbatim if printed.")
- **Source:** no published or retrievable account located.
