## Heard wording (clean transcript)
St. Thérèse says, prayer [possibly: love] is to be poured out into another.

## Evidence — verbatim from fetched sources
No source located matching this wording. Thérèse's classic definition of prayer (CCC ¶2558; Story of a Soul, Ms C 25r — see SRC-027) reads 'a surge of the heart... a simple look turned toward heaven' — no 'poured out into another.'

## Discrepancy / comparison notes
Wording cannot be matched to Thérèse's writings. The heard word itself is uncertain (ASR 'prayer' p=0.249 vs 'love'). The speaker's immediate context is kenosis ('to pour out' — his own gloss of the Greek), suggesting this may be his own image presented under Thérèse's name. Author must supply the source or the line should be recast.
