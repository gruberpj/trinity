## Evidence — verbatim from fetched sources
RSV: Do not be conformed to this world but be transformed by the renewal of your mind, that you may prove what is the will of God, what is good and acceptable and perfect. (RSV footnote: Greek age)

## Discrepancy / comparison notes
RSV prints 'this world' (footnote: Greek age); NABRE prints 'this age' ('Do not conform yourselves to this age'). Heard wording blends RSV 'Do not be conformed' with NABRE-style 'this age' — the author's Bible-edition choice (unresolved.md item F) applies here too.
