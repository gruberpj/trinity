## Authoritative text (Alexander 1889, stanzas 1-2; from The New English Hymnal #159 / hymnary.org)
"I bind unto myself today
The strong Name of the Trinity,
By invocation of the same,
The Three in One and One in Three.

I bind this day to me for ever,
By power of faith, Christ's Incarnation;
His baptism in the Jordan river;
His death on Cross for my salvation;
His bursting from the spicèd tomb;
His riding up the heavenly way;
His coming at the day of doom;
I bind unto myself today."

## Comparison notes
- DOCX text is authentically Alexander's translation. Hymnal variants: The Hymnal 1982 (Episcopal) #370 prints "spiced" (no accent) and "for ever," (comma); The New English Hymnal #159 prints "spicèd" and "for ever,". DOCX's period after "for ever." and lowercase "Christ's incarnation" match the Prayer Foundation web rendering.
- **Asterisk resolved:** "His coming at the day of doom;*" with the asterisk matches prayerfoundation.org's rendering (live since 2002; retrieved via Wayback 13 Dec 2015), whose footnote reads: "* Note: 'day of doom' is an Old English term meaning 'Day of Judgment.'" No printed hymnal carries the asterisk; it is a web-only gloss. If the book keeps the asterisk, supply that footnote with source attribution, or drop it.
- Public domain (translator died 1895).
