## Heard wording (clean transcript)
Recall John Paul II's Theology of the Body. He speaks often of the law of the gift. In this world, when we give something away... I no longer have this... But in the world of grace, when I give, I do not lose. In fact, I gain.

## Evidence — verbatim from fetched sources
JPII, TOB General Audience, 20 Feb 1980: That happens precisely by means of the sincere gift of himself. The exact phrase 'law of the gift' does NOT occur in vatican.va TOB audience texts (checked 14 Nov 1979 and 20 Feb 1980 audiences; site-wide search negative). CERC: This 'law of the gift,' as it is called by Catholic commentator George Weigel, is written in every human heart.

## Discrepancy / comparison notes
The phrase 'law of the gift' is George Weigel's popularization of John Paul II's 'sincere gift of self' teaching (itself rooted in Gaudium et spes §24 — see SRC-039), not a locatable phrase in the TOB audiences. If the book uses the phrase, attribute carefully (e.g., 'the "law of the gift," as it is commonly called in presentations of John Paul II's Theology of the Body').
