## Heard wording (clean transcript)
This is that simple glance directed towards heaven that St. Teresa [possibly: Thérèse] talks about when she says, 'What is prayer but a simple glance directed towards heaven?'

## Evidence — verbatim from fetched sources
CCC ¶2558 (official English): For me, prayer is a surge of the heart; it is a simple look turned toward heaven, it is a cry of recognition and of love, embracing both trial and joy. (fn. 1: St. Thérèse of Lisieux, Manuscrits autobiographiques, C 25r.) Taylor 1912 trans.: With me prayer is an uplifting of the heart; a glance towards heaven; a cry of gratitude and love, uttered equally in sorrow and in joy.

## Discrepancy / comparison notes
Heard wording is the speaker's rendering of 'a simple look turned toward heaven' (CCC ¶2558) / 'a glance towards heaven' (Taylor 1912) — matches no edition verbatim ('glance' = Taylor; 'towards heaven' = CCC). ALSO: the audio attribution itself is uncertain — ASR heard 'St. Teresa' and the C01 review flags [possibly: Thérèse]; the quotation is Thérèse of Lisieux's, not Teresa of Avila's. Human listen required before printing the attribution.
