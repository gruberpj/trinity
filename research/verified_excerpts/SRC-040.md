## Heard wording (clean transcript)
God the Father spoke one word from all eternity — St. John of the Cross says — and that word was his Son, and that word was uttered in silence. And if we are to receive that word, we are to receive it in silence.

## Evidence — verbatim from fetched sources
The Father spoke one Word, which was His Son, and this Word He speaks ever in eternal silence, and in silence must it be heard by the soul. (Counsels of Light and Love, saying no. 100, p. 106 of the 2007 edition per OCR; also cited elsewhere as 'Maxims on Love' no. 21 — numbering varies by edition; the same text block ends 'The whole world is not worthy...')

## Discrepancy / comparison notes
Speaker paraphrases from memory ('uttered in silence' for 'speaks ever in eternal silence, and in silence must it be heard by the soul'). Saying number varies by edition (#100 in the 2007 Counsels ed.; 'Maxims on Love' #21 in other printings). St. John of the Cross d. 1591 — the text is public domain; the 2007 typesetting and the ICS translation are ©.
