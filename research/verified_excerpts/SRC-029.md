## Heard wording (clean transcript)
St. Thérèse said at the end of her life, before she was about to expire from tuberculosis — a very painful, terrible disease — 'Everything is grace.'

## Evidence — verbatim from fetched sources
Novissima Verba (English ed. 1952), entry of 5 June 1897: Without doubt it is a great grace to receive the Sacraments, but when it does not please God to grant this favor, that is equally a grace... . Everything is a grace. (French original: Tout est grace.)

## Discrepancy / comparison notes
Fetched English rendering is 'Everything is a grace' (heard: 'Everything is grace' — article dropped). Date discrepancy: the 1952 edition places the passage under 5 June 1897; other editions cite 11 June 1897 — if a date is printed, check against the French Cahier jaune (archives-carmel-lisieux.fr, Cloudflare-blocked to the pipeline).
