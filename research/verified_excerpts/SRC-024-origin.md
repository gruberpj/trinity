## Verdict

The exact wording "You are not made for comfort. You are made for greatness." was located in **no official Benedict XVI text**. Every Cologne WYD 2005 text aimed at young people, all Aug 2005 Angelus addresses, and the Aug 2005 homilies were fetched and searched for comfort/greatness (and the German Bequemlichkeit/Komfort/Größe) — zero relevant hits. The sentence circulates only as an unsourced/misattributed paraphrase (quote-aggregator sites; debunked in Catholic blogs). The line is a FOCUS-style condensation of genuine Benedict themes, for which two real official sentences exist (quoted below) — most notably *Spe Salvi* §33.

## Closest genuine official sentences (verbatim, fetched 2026-09-16)

1. **Benedict XVI, *Spe Salvi* (encyclical, 30 Nov 2007), §33** — https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_ben-xvi_enc_20071130_spe-salvi.html (accessed 2026-09-16):
   > "Man was created for greatness—for God himself; he was created to be filled by God. But his heart is too small for the greatness to which it is destined. It must be stretched."

2. **Homily, Mass at Marienfeld, XX World Youth Day, 21 Aug 2005** — https://www.vatican.va/content/benedict-xvi/en/homilies/2005/documents/hf_ben-xvi_hom_20050821_20th-world-youth-day.html (accessed 2026-09-16):
   > "It may be comfortable, but at times of crisis we are left to ourselves."
   > "Living and acting thus, we will soon realize that it is much better to be useful and at the disposal of others than to be concerned only with the comforts that are offered to us."

3. **Rule-out notes (hits that are NOT the line):** the 21 Aug 2005 homily's single "greatness" is "…we must learn to grasp it ever more deeply… Let us discover the intimate riches of the Church's liturgy and its true greatness: it is not we who are celebrating for ourselves…"; the 18 Aug 2005 welcoming ceremony's single "greatness" is "…we can always encounter the inconceivable greatness of a God who humbled himself even to appearing in a manger…". Neither supports the "made for greatness" attribution.

## Comparison to the heard paraphrase

- Heard: "you are not made for comfort. You are made for greatness."
- No official sentence says this. The theme (comfort vs. greatness; man created for greatness) is genuinely Benedict's (*Spe Salvi* §33; the 21 Aug 2005 homily's comfort sentences). The heard line is best described as a FOCUS-circles condensation of these themes, not a quotation.
- Circulation pattern: unsourced attributions on quote sites (Goodreads etc.); debunking discussions exist on Catholic blogs (e.g., catholicbiblestudent.com 2014 "Made for Greatness?"; thecatholicgentleman.com 2023 — pages fetched by pipeline subagent 2026-09-16, text extraction not re-verified).
