## Heard wording (clean transcript)
'Let us create,' he says. 'Let there be light.' Fiat lux, he creates. And there was light. 'Let there be dry land.'

## Evidence — verbatim from fetched sources
RSV Gen 1:3: And God said, 'Let there be light'; and there was light. RSV Gen 1:9: And God said, 'Let the waters under the heavens be gathered together into one place, and let the dry land appear.' And it was so. Nova Vulgata Gen 1:3: Dixitque Deus: 'Fiat lux'. Et facta est lux. (Gen 1:26 'Let us make man...' — see SRC-047.)

## Discrepancy / comparison notes
'Let there be light'/'let the dry land appear' match RSV; 'Fiat lux' verified in the Nova Vulgata. 'Let us create' is the speaker's rendering of Gen 1:26 (SRC-047). Note: the speaker calls the creation verbs 'the passive tense' — grammatically loose (fiat is jussive/passive; 'Let us make' is cohortative plural) — flagged for the theological reviewer in the C03 review log (Q9).
