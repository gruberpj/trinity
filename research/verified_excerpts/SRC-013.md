## Facts verified
- Artist: Andrei Rublev. Medium: tempera on wood panel. Dimensions: 142 × 114 cm. Collection: State Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow (inventory no. 13012).
- Dating is genuinely disputed among authorities: c. 1410 (Britannica), "1411 or 1425-27" (Tretyakov/Wikipedia), 1425-1427 (Tretyakov Gallery Magazine).
- **Provenance update:** in 2023 the icon was transferred from the Tretyakov Gallery to the custody of the Russian Orthodox Church and displayed at the Cathedral of Christ the Saviour, Moscow (per Britannica/Wikipedia).
