## Heard wording (clean transcript)
It's like the woman who was the widow who gave only two small coins in the treasury. And our Lord, on the eve of his passion, said, that woman has given more than everyone else. Everyone else gave of their excess. She gave from her poverty, her whole livelihood.

## Evidence — verbatim from fetched sources
RSV Mk 12:43-44: And he called his disciples to him, and said to them, 'Truly, I say to you, this poor widow has put in more than all those who are contributing to the treasury. For they all contributed out of their abundance; but she out of her poverty has put in everything she had, her whole living.' RSV Lk 21:3-4: And he said, 'Truly I tell you, this poor widow has put in more than all of them; for they all contributed out of their abundance, but she out of her poverty put in all the living that she had.'

## Discrepancy / comparison notes
Retelling, not a quotation. Speaker's setting 'on the eve of his passion' fits Luke's Jerusalem context (Lk 21:1-4). Kept as heard.
