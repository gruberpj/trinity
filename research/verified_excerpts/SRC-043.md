## Heard wording (clean transcript)
St. John Henry Newman — himself was an Oratorian priest like myself — he said that watching is that feeling you have when you're expecting a friend to come to your house, and for some reason he's delayed, but all of your thoughts are fixed on him...

## Evidence — verbatim from fetched sources
Do you know the feeling in matters of this life, of expecting a friend, expecting him to come, and he delays? ... To watch for Christ is a feeling such as all these; as far as feelings of this world are fit to shadow out those of another. (PPS vol. IV, sermon 22, 'Watching', on Mark xiii. 33)

Heard paraphrase accurately captures the passage. Also confirmed in the same sermon: 'He is behind this material framework; earth and sky are but a veil going between Him and us' (the veil image the speaker merges into the Apologia retelling — see SRC-042).
