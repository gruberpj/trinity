## Heard wording (clean transcript)
He said that mysteries — what distinguishes them from problems — is that mysteries implicate the self. You are involved in the mystery. ... Gabriel Marcel said that many of the problems of the 20th century, with the massive amounts of death on scales never before envisioned, it came from treating mysteries as problems. / E01: Gabriel Marcel said that mystery, properly speaking, involves the viewer. We are part of it. ... He distinguished problem from mystery.

## Evidence — verbatim from fetched sources
(p. 171) A mystery is a problem which encroaches upon its own data and invades them, and so is transcended qua problem. (p. 116) But a mystery is something in which I am myself involved, and it can therefore only be thought of as a sphere where the distinction between what is in me and what is before me loses its meaning and its initial validity. (Metaphysical Diary, Feb. 2nd) mystery is a problem that encroaches upon the intrinsic conditions of its own possibility (and not upon its data).
