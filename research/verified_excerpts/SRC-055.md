## Heard wording (clean transcript)
...or Louis de Montfort understood, in his devotion to Mary. When we give everything to Jesus through Mary, we don't lose anything of ourselves, but we gain.

## Evidence — verbatim from fetched sources
Faber trans. (1863), p. 87: We renounce, as is expressed in the formula of consecration, the devil, the world, sin, and self; and we give ourselves entirely to Jesus Christ by the hands of Mary. Act of Consecration (pp. 191-192): I deliver and consecrate to thee, as thy slave, my body and soul, my goods, both interior and exterior, and even the value of all my good actions, past, present, and future... 1949 numbered ed.: ...consecrated and sacrificed to Jesus Christ through Mary can no longer dispose of the value of any of...

## Discrepancy / comparison notes
Heard 'give everything to Jesus through Mary' is not verbatim; Faber renders 'we give ourselves entirely to Jesus Christ by the hands of Mary' (p. 87), the 1949 edition uses 'through Mary.' Total consecration theme confirmed. De Montfort d. 1716 — public domain.
