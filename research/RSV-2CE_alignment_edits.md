# RSV-2CE Alignment Edits — "Mystery of the Trinity"

- **Prepared:** 2026-09-17
- **Source:** `research/RSV-2CE_verification_worksheet.md` (rows 1–40) + exact strings re-verified against `chapters/drafts/` (read-only; no chapter files were modified).
- **Policy (owner):** printed Scripture should match **RSV-2CE** unless the spoken text deviates substantially; this alignment supersedes the heard-modernization policy **only where 2CE is digitally confirmed** (biblia.com previews, 2026-09-17).
- **Application rule:** replace ONLY the quoted Scripture words; speaker prose, attributions, quotation marks, and surrounding punctuation structure stay as printed. `[quotation wording unverified]` tags stay (owner may retire a tag once its wording is adopted).
- **Mechanics:** each Find string includes enough surrounding context to be unique and find-and-replace-safe. All chapter files use straight double quotes `"` and em dashes `—` (verified).

### 1.1 `chapters/drafts/01_icons_of_the_trinity.md` — 6 edits

**E01 — Mt 28:18 (worksheet row 1)**
- Find: `"All power in heaven and on earth has been given to me."`
- Replace: `"All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me."`
- Note: one-word variant ("power" → "authority"; 2CE = RSV here). Previously kept as heard (SRC-019).

**E02 — Gen 1:26 (row 2)**
- Find: `when God says, "Let us create man in our own image and likeness" [quotation wording unverified]`
- Replace: `when God says, "Let us make man in our image, after our likeness" [quotation wording unverified]`
- Note: memory paraphrase (SRC-047). 2CE "Let us make man in our image, after our likeness;…" — replacement ends at "likeness" (book's quote span).

**E03 — Gen 1:26 fragment, same paragraph (row 2)**
- Find: `the choirs of angels — "Let us create," God would speak to them.`
- Replace: `the choirs of angels — "Let us make," God would speak to them.`
- Note: keeps the quoted fragment consistent with E02.

**E04 — Lk 2:49 (row 4)**
- Find: `"Do you not know I must be about my Father's business and my Father's house" [quotation wording unverified]`
- Replace: `"Did you not know that I must be in my Father's house" [quotation wording unverified]`
- Note: KJV-tradition blend (SRC-048). 2CE ends the verse with "?" — book's quote has no terminal punctuation; owner may add "?" inside the closing quote if desired.

**E05 — Jn 12:24 (row 5)**
- Find: `"Unless a seed fall to the ground and dies, it remains but a seed. But if it dies, it produces much fruit" [quotation wording unverified]`
- Replace: `"Unless a grain of wheat falls into the earth and dies, it remains alone; but if it dies, it bears much fruit" [quotation wording unverified]`
- Note: heavier rewrite of a memory paraphrase (SRC-049). 2CE begins the verse "Truly, truly, I say to you, unless…" — "Truly, truly, I say to you," is not in the printed span; owner may prepend it.

**E06 — Gen 1:31 (row 8)**
- Find: `He looked at us and said, "You are very good" [quotation wording unverified]`
- Replace: `He looked at us and said, "It was very good" [quotation wording unverified]`
- Note: direct-address adaptation (SRC-061). 2CE: "And God saw everything that he had made, and behold, it was very good." — replacement is the matching span; capital "It" keeps the book's quote-start casing (2CE prints lowercase "it" mid-verse).

## 2. Suspect rows — alignment edits + CONFIRM WITH OWNER

These 6 suspect readings are confirmed in 2CE but **contradict the heard audio**, so each aligned edit must be confirmed by the owner before applying. 2CE wording included per instruction.

**S01 — Ps 42:3 "?" (row 11, `03_gift_and_liturgy.md`)**
- Find: `"Where is your God?" These things I remember`
- Replace: `"Where is your God." These things I remember`
- 2CE confirmed: v3 ends with a **period** inside the quotes ("Where is your God."), not "?".

**S02 — Ps 42:5 "Savior" capitalization (row 11, `03_gift_and_liturgy.md`)**
- Find: `for I shall again praise him, my Savior and my God.`
- Replace: `for I shall again praise him, my savior and my God.`
- 2CE confirmed: lowercase "savior" (biblia preview truncated at "my savior"; "and my God" continuation kept as printed).

**S03 — Rom 12:2 "this age" (row 12, `03_gift_and_liturgy.md`)**
- Find: `says, "Do not be conformed to this age, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind."`
- Replace: `says, "Do not be conformed to this world but be transformed by the renewal of your mind."`
- 2CE confirmed: "this world" NOT "this age" (NABRE reading; SRC-046 blend). Comma after "world" dropped per 2CE.

**S04 — Rom 12:2 "this age" (row 21, `04_relationship.md`)**
- Find: `St. Paul says in his letter to the Romans, "Do not be conformed to this age, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind."`
- Replace: `St. Paul says in his letter to the Romans, "Do not be conformed to this world but be transformed by the renewal of your mind."`

**S05 — Mt 28:18 self-correction (row 30, `06_evangelization.md`)**
- Find: `"All power in heaven and earth — all authority in heaven and earth has been given over to me.`
- Replace: `"All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me.`
- 2CE confirmed: "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me." — collapses the heard self-correction; "on earth" (not "in earth").

**S06 — Mt 28:19 "Go, therefore" (row 30, `06_evangelization.md`)**
- Find: `Go, therefore, make disciples of all nations`
- Replace: `Go therefore and make disciples of all nations`

**S07 — Mt 28:20 "even to the close" (row 30, `06_evangelization.md`)**
- Find: `Behold, I am with you always, even to the close of the age."`
- Replace: `Behold, I am with you always, to the close of the age."`
- Note: "Behold" already matches 2CE (RSV/RSVCE print "lo") — no change to that word.

**S08 — Mt 11:28 "heavily burdened" (row 39, `epilogue_engineering_mystery.md`)**
- Find: `"Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest."`
- Replace: `"Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest."`
- 2CE confirmed: "heavy laden" NOT "heavily burdened" (SRC-053 modernization).
